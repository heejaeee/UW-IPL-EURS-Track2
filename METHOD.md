# UW-IPL — EURS Track2, T1v100, submission 19405

The owner confirmed T1v100, submission #19405 (User #2062), as the final testing model; official Composite is 0.401. config.json selects T1v100. onnx/submitted_model.onnx is the unchanged original T1v100 artifact, SHA-256 95daf709723278a946e5416c83b45576b9a5b0ac3d989f8b85df1b4b6a0a4369. v104/#19412 matched the reported official metrics and was not adopted. The published reproduction therefore retains the T1v100 guided filter (radii 16 and 4, epsilon 0.001).

A frozen StatisticalRestorerV3S v5 predicts mixtures of TRAIN-fitted degradation conditions and image-conditioned color coefficients. A separable luminance unsharp mask (k=0.8) produces restored_image. Segmentation uses the first 15 MobileNetV2 blocks (dilated OS8), LR-ASPP and inherited normalization. T1 evaluates a whole 384×512 image and its horizontal flip with shared weights, then unflips and averages logits. There are no tiles. Two RGB guided-filter stages (r=16 then 4, epsilon=0.001), guided by the post-USM restoration, refine seg_logits. An LDL-transpose covariance solve uses separable box pooling. Static input: 1×3×1024×1024; official compute: 11.43190293 GFLOPs and 0.889754 M initializer parameters.

Both candidates descend from v69→v91→v92. v91 used 1,000 steps of class-balanced Track1 v1 logit distillation. v92 starts at v91-1000, uses OTHER CE weight 3, inverse-frequency KD cap 10, KL temperature 2/weight 1, and a 4,000-step cosine schedule with peak encoder/head LR 3e-7/3e-6. T1 uses its 3,000-step EMA. T1v100 continues that checkpoint for 1,000 steps, retaining the 4,000-step LR horizon, and adds aligned frozen SAM3 feature cosine loss (weight 0.5) through a training-only 96→256 projector. The projector is absent from export. BN statistics, restorer, USM and input-mix parameters remain fixed.

FACTSHEET.json enumerates TRAIN/selection scenes and external assets. The 5,481-image KD pool contains EURS TRAIN 3,200, allowed Dev 1,000, SUIM 912 and CoralscapesV2 369. starfish1 (200 Dev images) is excluded from training and used only for teacher-agreement evaluation, not GT accuracy. Dev pseudo-label/KD use is disclosed. The inherited E034 teacher saw ray1/seahorse1, limiting local validation independence. Testing images are excluded from training and target generation in this final lineage. Earlier failed research is summarized in EXPERIMENTS.md. Inference takes RGB only; filename metadata is used only in teacher training/synthesis.

Inherited student supervision and restoration sources are also disclosed. [DINOv3-L/16 LVD-1689M](https://huggingface.co/facebook/dinov3-vitl16-pretrain-lvd1689m), under the [DINOv3 License](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov3/blob/main/LICENSE.md), was a frozen, training-only dense-feature teacher in v4 (cosine loss weight 0.25), inherited through v5/v9 into v17 and the final student; it is not deployed and is separate from the later SAM3 feature loss in v100. The recorded direct coral source is [CoralscapesV2 TRAIN](https://huggingface.co/datasets/josauder/coralscapesV2), which extends [original Coralscapes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20000); the audit found no separate v1 dataset or v1 pretrained model use in the final lineage. The Track2 contributor restorer also used SUID [Hou et al., IEEE Access 2020; DOI [10.21227/agdr-y109](https://doi.org/10.21227/agdr-y109)]: 29 of the 30 clean ground-truth images (GTI) used for non-commercial academic research, in accordance with the dataset terms.

export_onnx.py reconstructs local weights, exports opset 17 and simplifies the graph. It never downloads or copies the submitted ONNX to pass comparison. Both candidates passed graph/weight comparison in the offline PyTorch 2.4 container. The only cross-version export adjustment renames one folded shape constant without changing its value or operators. See reports for export attributes, numerical checks and historical validation records. This public repository reproduces the confirmed leaderboard submission; export itself performs no leaderboard upload.
